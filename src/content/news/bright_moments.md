## Bright Moments at the Hall

![Concert flyer](../../img/2026-08-28 Concert .png)

Bright Moments at the Hall is a ticketed event with seating limited to 80. We suggest a donation of $25 per seat, but we ask that you give what you can. 
Those who can give more will make this event accessible to all and help ensure future community concerts.

Ed Hrybyk grew up on Cedar Ave. during the 1990s. He was introduced to the bass at Relay Elementary School. 
He last performed on the Town Hall stage with Ultrafaux in 2016. During the pandemic Ed initiated the Baltimore Pop Up Jazz Jam and the Charm City Porch Concerts to bring live jazz to Baltimore communities. 
Both continue to this day.

Ed’s efforts to bring live music to the people of Baltimore City have been recognized by Baltimore Magazine, Maryland State Arts Council, Baltimore Office of the Promotion of the Arts, Fox45, WJZ-13, and WMAR-2. In December 2021, Ed was given the distinct honor of being a featured performer with the Baltimore Symphony Orchestra.

We are happy to host the Ed Hrybyk Sextet performing tunes from his original album, “Bright Moments.” I hope to see you there.
